## ANOTHER ATTEMPT

Another attempt at blogging on my own — after the long-defunct
[jogger.pl](https://jogger.pl), and after posting under my own
domain using WordPress for a while, here we are again.

## WHY AGAIN

No real reason beyond wanting a place to write things down that
isn't tied to a platform I don't control. This time it's a
hand-built teletext-styled static site instead of WordPress —
markdown files, no database, no plugins to update.

## WHAT TO EXPECT

Mostly notes on whatever I'm building or breaking at the time:
homelab, self-hosting, DevOps, and lately AI tooling.
