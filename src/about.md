## WHO

Krzysztof Lewczuk — DevOps / Cloud / AI / SysOps, based in Wrocław,
Poland.

I build and run the infrastructure underneath products: pipelines,
clusters, observability, and the automation that keeps them boring
in a good way.
Currently Senior DevOps Engineer at **Thaumatec Tech
Group**, and running my own thing as HTSH on the side.

## HOW IT STARTED

I've been fascinated by computers for as long as I can remember.
When I started elementary school, around the time Windows 95 was
released, I wanted an Amiga. A friend had the floppy-disk version,
and playing Mortal Kombat on it seemed incredible compared to the
"Pegasus" I had at home.

The school had a computer club, where I first got my hands on
MS-DOS and tools like Norton Commander and Lotus 1-2-3 — and, of
course, games like Hercules. Not exactly a traditional introduction
to computing, but it stuck.

My first attempts at making websites date back to late 2003, when I
discovered HTML through Paweł Wimmer's course. They were made at an
internet café, since I didn't get my first computer with an
internet connection until 2005. Some of those early experiments are
still preserved somewhere in the Wayback Machine. I strongly advise
against looking them up.

By late 2006, I had become user #434935 on the Linux Counter. From
there, things escalated quickly. I spent years breaking and fixing
my systems in pursuit of the perfect desktop, remastered my own
Linux distribution based on Ubuntu 6.06, and contributed a little
to Polish distributions such as PLD Linux and the now-defunct
KateOS.

Apart from a few small webmastering jobs and fixing computers for
family and friends, it was still just a hobby. That changed in
2013, when I started working as an IT Specialist.

## WHAT I DO

- Design and operate CI/CD pipelines, from bare-metal runners to
  managed cloud services
- Build and maintain cloud and on-prem infrastructure with IaC
- Wire up observability so incidents get caught before they page
  anyone
- Apply lightweight AI/ML where it actually removes toil, not where
  it adds a demo

## SELF-HOSTING

Outside work I run a homelab and self-host whatever I can get away
with — Proxmox, a small K3s cluster, my own CI runners, this site.
If a service has a self-hosted alternative, odds are I've already
tried running it myself.

## EXPLORING AI

Currently digging into the AI space beyond the hype: local models
with llama.cpp and MLX, automation with n8n, routing with LiteLLM,
image workflows with ComfyUI. Early days, learning in public.

## KNOWLEDGE SHARING

I also run internal knowledge sharing at Thaumatec — SKS, regular
in-house presentations, and sKS, shorter Anki-card notes on tools
and Linux basics. Type 303 for details.

## OUTSIDE OF WORK

As a fan of fantasy and sci-fi, I'm fascinated by other worlds,
whether I find them in books, games, or real places that look like
they came straight out of a story. I love exploring old castles and
palaces, and I'm an avid reader and book collector. When it comes
to games, I enjoy everything from MMOs and action RPGs to casual
mobile and browser games, with classics like Sudoku and Mahjong
being perfect for a quick mental break.

Somehow, these hobbies tend to turn into projects of their own:

- castles became a map (306),
- books became an inventory app (307),
- games became a couple of ad-free apps I built myself (304).
